YOUR ODDS DON'T IMPROVE.

⚠ YOUR NEXT CHEST NEVER GETS BETTER ODDS.

Opening 100,000 chests without a win does not increase the next chest's probability. Treasure Hour temporarily doubles the same fixed chance per eligible chest, not progressively. What increases is your unconditional cumulative chance of having won at least once across all those attempts. There is no assumed pity system.
